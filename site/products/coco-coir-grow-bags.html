Questions

Asked and answered.

How do I start a bag?

Open the top, then soak it all the way. Pour water in slowly until the coir has expanded and is evenly moist, wait 3 to 5 minutes, level it off and plant.

1 gallon or 2 gallon?

One plant per bag. 1 gallon suits herbs, lettuce and strawberries; 2 gallon gives peppers and small tomatoes more root room.

Can I return an opened bag?

Once a bag has been opened or the coir watered it cannot be resold, so it is not eligible for a standard return. Unopened packs follow the 30-day policy.

Is it peat-free?

Yes, 0 % peat. Coco coir is made from coconut husk.
