Questions

Asked and answered.

How do the panels connect?

A flat tab on one panel drops into the slot on the next and snaps. Build a runway as long as your row.

Square or connector panel?

Connector panels are long and narrow for rows of planters; the square panel sits under a single pot.

Can I store them?

Yes, the panels nest and stack flat.
